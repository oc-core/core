# Project governance

This document describes how decisions are made in Open Communities Core today, and how that is
meant to change as the project grows. It is itself subject to change through the process it
describes.

## Today: a maintainer-led project

- The maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) make technical decisions.
- Decisions that affect contributors are recorded as Architecture Decision Records in
  [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/). Anyone can propose an ADR; a maintainer accepts it.
- Discussion happens in the open: issues, pull requests and ADRs. Private channels are used
  only for security reports and conduct matters.
- Disagreements are resolved by discussion; if none is reached, the lead maintainer decides and
  records the reasoning.

## Roles

| Role | How you get it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor | a merged pull request | listed in the changelog; your voice counts in discussions |
| Reviewer | sustained contributions; invited by maintainers | can approve pull requests |
| Maintainer | sustained reviewing; consensus of existing maintainers | merge rights, release rights, ADR acceptance |

## Where this is going

When the project has more than two active maintainers from more than one organisation, this
document is revised to introduce a steering group, voting rules for ADRs and a process for
adding and removing maintainers. Legal ownership of the name and trademarks, and the entity
that receives funding, are separate questions from technical governance and are documented when
they change.

## Funding

OCC may receive grants. Grant deliverables are planned and tracked in the open (issue milestones
in this repository). Funding does not buy decision rights; it buys the maintainers' time to
deliver what is planned.
